Playground

Every GalleryOptions/plugin-option field as a live form below the gallery — flip settings, pick dynamic (JSON items) vs. selector (DOM markup) mode, and the gallery rebuilds itself immediately, no separate step. All 24 items are generated inline (SVG placeholders, one real HTML5 video, one YouTube link, one Vimeo link) — no external image assets, so this page has no dependency beyond dist/ and one small sample clip.
